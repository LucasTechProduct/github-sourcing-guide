A short, hand-picked list of well-known repositories per technology. These are obvious anchors, useful as a starting point for [Method 2](README.md#method-2-start-from-repositories-the-strong-signal). They are not an exhaustive index. Build your own list for the exact stack you hire for.

Feed any `owner/repo` from this list to [`snippets/repo_contributors.py`](snippets/repo_contributors.py) to pull its contributors.
